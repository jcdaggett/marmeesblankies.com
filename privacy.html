Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you shop, sign up, or leave a review with us, we collect the information you give us directly:

  • Your name, email, and shipping address, when you place an order (collected and processed by Stripe, our payment processor)
  • Your email address, if you sign up for updates or a discount code
  • Your name, rating, review text, and any photos you choose to upload, if you leave a review

We also automatically collect some technical information when you visit the site, described below under Cookies & Tracking.

How we use it

  • To process and ship your order
  • To respond to questions or requests you send us
  • To send you updates or offers, only if you've signed up to receive them
  • To display approved customer reviews on the site
  • To understand which marketing channels are bringing customers to the site, and to show relevant ads

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & tracking

The site uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Necessary cookies — used to run the site itself, like remembering you've already seen a popup during your visit. These don't require your consent.
  • Attribution cookie — remembers which link or channel first brought you to the site (for example, an ad or a social media post), so we can understand what's working. Stored for up to 90 days.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel — helps us understand site activity and show relevant ads on Meta's platforms. Some purchase information is also shared with Meta directly from our server in a privacy-preserving way (your email is one-way hashed, never sent in plain text).

You'll see a cookie banner on your first visit where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies (attribution and Meta Pixel). Necessary cookies always apply, since the site can't function properly without them.

Who we share information with

  • Stripe — processes payments and stores your order and payment details securely. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the website and stores review photos and submitted email addresses.
  • Meta — receives limited, privacy-preserving purchase and browsing data if you accept non-essential cookies, used for advertising.

Your choices

  • You can decline non-essential cookies at any time from the cookie banner.
  • You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • You can ask us to delete your personal information by emailing us — see below.

Contact us
